The Findings That Matter

1. You don't actually own your website

It's built on GoHighLevel, on the previous agency's account. That is exactly why you can't get into it and why two months of back-and-forth got nowhere. It was never set up in your name. Rebuilt on your own account, no agency going under can ever lock you out again.

2. Nothing filters your leads

Wrong postcodes, 2am enquiries, six hours quoting price-finders, and house-buyers wanting a free inspection to knock money off a seller. Every one reaches you because the form asks nothing that would screen them. A postcode gate and job-type qualifier stop most of it before it touches your day.

3. Your best proof is sitting unused

The photos of your team, your vans and your finished roofs are genuinely strong, and they build more trust than any headline. Right now they're barely on the site. This is the one area you're already winning, it just isn't being shown.

4. No reviews are being collected

You said it yourself: get the reviews and you're top of the list. There's no system asking happy customers after a finished job, and no reviews displayed, so the thing that decides your Google map ranking isn't happening.

5. The good news: the demand and the budget are already there

You're booked four days a week, you have big contracts through October, and you were already spending around £1,000 a week on advertising. Nothing here needs more money. It needs the leaks closed and the spend pointed at genuine jobs.
